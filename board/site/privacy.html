Props Board

Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Props Board (this website and its Android app) shows sports model picks, player props and a parlay builder. It is an information tool only. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to any sportsbook account.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics or tracking in the site or the app, and no personal information is collected, sold or shared.

What stays on your device

Your settings and saved parlay slips are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave it, and clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

Other services

  • The pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the standard request details (such as your IP address) when fonts load.
  • The site is hosted on Render, which keeps standard server logs.
  • "Open in DraftKings" links take you to DraftKings' own site or app, where DraftKings' privacy policy applies.

Age and responsible play

This content is for adults of legal betting age in their location. Picks are model estimates, not guarantees. If gambling stops being fun, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the app's Google Play listing.
